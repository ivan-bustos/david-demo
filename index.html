Dragonfruit + Yuzu sparkling soda

GlorbitA tiny planet of fizz.

Juicy pink dragonfruit, a zap of yuzu and a pinch of sea salt, all orbiting one very cold can. Crack it open and the bubbles do the rest.

Taste the orbit→ Meet the can

What’s in orbit

Three flavors, one gravitational pull

  • Dragonfruit

    The juicy, berry-ish heart of the can. Bright pink, gently sweet, the kind of flavor that makes you look at the can twice.

  • Yuzu

    A citrus zap somewhere between grapefruit and lime, landing right after the first sip like a little meteor.

  • Sea-salt sparkle

    A pinch of salt and lively bubbles keep everything crisp, so the fruit tastes brighter and the finish stays clean.

Flavor descriptions are creative copy for an imaginary drink, not a recipe, ingredient list or nutrition information.

Designed to be spotted

A can that thinks it’s a planet

  • Hot-pink shell with dragonfruit-seed speckles, so it pops from across the fridge.
  • A wavy yuzu-yellow band that wraps around like a planetary ring.
  • The orbit logo: one dark little planet with two bubble moons.

About this demo

Glorbit is an invented brand created to show a playful landing page. There is no real drink, company, store or delivery behind it.

  • Nothing here is for sale, and there’s no checkout or sign-up.
  • The flavor, can and moments are fiction, not health, nutrition or ingredient claims.
  • Any resemblance to a real soda brand is unintentional.
